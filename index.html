The idea behind Maarifa

What if great learning never had to stop at the classroom door?

Maarifa gives Form 1 through Form 6 students a simpler way to build consistent study routines, find reliable learning materials, and get support from qualified teachers outside class hours.

For educators, it’s a place to publish useful study materials, understand how students engage with them, and earn monthly revenue based on verified engagement. One thoughtful lesson can reach learners wherever they are.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Maarifa collects, uses, and protects information when you use our learning platform. We handle information to provide the service and support students and educators.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Maarifa, information may include your full name, phone number used for account and payment processing, student grade or Form level, and educational materials you upload as an educator. We may also process transaction records and service activity needed to operate accounts, verify payments, and understand material engagement.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide access to study resources; organize learning materials by subject and level; process or verify subscriptions and payouts; calculate engagement-based revenue splits for educators; maintain and improve the service; and respond to support requests.

3. When information is shared

We share the minimum information needed with payment gateway aggregators, including AzamPay, strictly to verify transactions and process subscriptions or educator payouts. Payment information is also handled by the relevant mobile money operator under its own terms. We do not authorize payment providers to use Maarifa account information for unrelated purposes through our integration.

4. Security practices

We use encrypted data transmission and Firebase security rules to help protect information and limit access. We apply access controls appropriate to operating the service. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please protect your account credentials and contact us if you suspect a security issue.

5. Your rights and data retention

You may request access to or an export of your account data, or request account deletion, by emailing maarifayetutanzania@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes, and comply with legal or financial recordkeeping requirements. Where information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact maarifayetutanzania@gmail.com.
